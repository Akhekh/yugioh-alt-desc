You can only use each of the following effects of "Chaos Summoning Beast" once per turn:
  • You can Tribute this card; Special Summon 1 "Uria, Lord of Searing Flames", "Hamon, Lord of Striking Thunder", or "Raviel, Lord of Phantasms", from your hand, ignoring its Summoning conditions.
  • You can banish this card from your GY; add 1 "Fallen Paradise" from your Deck to your hand.
